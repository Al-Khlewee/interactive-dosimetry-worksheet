Interactive Dosimetry Worksheet

Absorbed dose to water calibration, based on the IAEA TRS-398 Code of Practice

How to use this worksheet

  1. Pick the beam type using the tabs below (photon or electron).
  2. Fill in sections 1–3 in order: beam and set-up, chamber and calibration certificate, then your measured readings.
  3. Check the results on the right. Every correction factor and the final dose update as you type.
  4. Print the report when you are done. It includes both worksheets.

3. Measured Readings

Polarity Correction (kpol)

Take readings at your usual voltage V1 with both polarities.

The voltage you normally use for measurements.
Recombination Correction (ks)

Two-voltage method: take one reading at V1 and one at a lower voltage V2. A V1/V2 ratio of 2–5 is supported; 3 is typical.

Your main reading (usual polarity and voltage). The dose is based on it.
