04Correct the reading

Fix today's conditions

Air density · kTP

Warm air or low pressure leaves less air in the chamber, so the reading drops.

kTP = 273.2 + T273.2 + T0 × P0P
Measure T, P · Certificate T0, P01.0094

Polarity · kpol

The chamber reads slightly differently at + and − voltage. Measure both and average.

kpol = |M+| + |M−|2M
Measure M+, M− at both polarities1.0000

Corrections bring the reading back to the conditions of calibration.

MQ = M1 × kTP × kelec × kpol × ks
