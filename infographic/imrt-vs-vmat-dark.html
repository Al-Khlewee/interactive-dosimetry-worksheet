Radiotherapy delivery techniques

IMRT vs VMAT: same goal, different motion

Both modulate the beam to wrap the dose around the tumour and spare healthy organs. The difference is how the linac moves.

IMRT

Fixed beams 5–9 gantry angles

VMAT

Rotating arc 1–2 arcs around the patient

Machine QA demands

VMAT also needs dynamic tests of everything moving together.

Same calibration underneath. Both techniques deliver monitor units that were calibrated by TRS-398 in a static 10×10 cm² field. An error in the reference calibration shifts every IMRT and VMAT plan equally. Both also need patient-specific QA before treatment.
Other arc names. IMAT was the early multi-arc form of arc IMRT. RapidArc (Varian) and VMAT/SmartArc (Elekta, Philips) are vendor names for VMAT. Tomotherapy delivers IMRT in a helical arc with a binary MLC.
