The workflow in five steps
STEP 1

Measure beam quality

Photons: TPR20,10
Electrons: R50 (g/cm²)

STEP 2

Set up in water

Chamber at zref, 10×10 cm² field, SSD or SAD of 100 cm

STEP 3

Take readings

M1, M+ / M−, M2 at V2, plus air T and P

STEP 4

Apply corrections

kTP · kelec · kpol · ks turn M1 into MQ

STEP 5

Calculate dose

× ND,w × kQ gives Gy/MU; × MU/min gives the dose rate

The corrections that turn M1 into MQ Each one is close to 1. A large deviation usually means a measurement problem.
kTP

Air density

The chamber is open to the air. Scales the reading back to the reference temperature and pressure on the certificate.
273.2 + T273.2 + T0 × P0P
Typical0.97 – 1.03
kpol

Polarity

The reading changes slightly when the chamber voltage is reversed. Averages both polarities.
|M+| + |M−|2 M1
Typical0.998 – 1.002
ks

Ion recombination

Some ions recombine before they are collected. Uses the two-voltage method (V1/V2 between 2 and 5).
a0 + a1M1M2 + a2(M1M2)²
Typical1.000 – 1.010

Common pitfalls
Let the chamber settleAllow thermal equilibrium in the water and pre-irradiate the chamber before recording readings.
Watch the unitsPressure in kPa, temperature in °C, readings per MU. Mixing hPa or nC per 100 MU shifts the result.
Stay inside the tableskQ and the ks coefficients are only valid within their tabulated ranges. Don't extrapolate.
