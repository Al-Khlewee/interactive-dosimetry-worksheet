THE SETUP

Chamber in water at the reference depth

The beam enters a water tank at a fixed distance (SSD or SAD 100 cm) with a 10×10 cm² field. The chamber sits at zref: 10 g/cm² for photons, 0.6·R50 − 0.1 for electrons.

THE MEASUREMENT

Inside the ionization chamber

Radiation knocks electrons out of the air in the cavity, creating ion pairs. The voltage pulls them apart: one sign to the wall, the other to the collector. The electrometer adds up that charge as the reading M.

THE ELECTROMETER

How the tiny chamber current becomes a reading

The high voltage on the chamber wall sweeps the ions out. The collector current (picoamps to nanoamps) flows down the signal wire of a triaxial cable into an op-amp integrator, whose input sits at virtual ground. The charge piles up on the feedback capacitor Cf, so the output voltage is proportional to the collected charge. The ADC digitises it and the display shows M in nC. The reset switch empties Cf before each measurement.
